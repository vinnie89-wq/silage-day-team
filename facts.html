📌 Key facts for the app

Everything the visitor app says about the date, times, place, charities and prices comes from here. The master copy is the Key assumptions and details register: change it there first, then here. Each box shows its register ID.

Sign in with your organiser Google account.

The day

A5 Charities

Shown on the home page (as logos, if you add them) and the Raffle page, linked to their websites. Leave a row empty to remove it.

Logos: use each charity's own "in aid of" logo – ask their fundraising team for it, and for their OK to use it. Don't take one off their website. PNG, JPG or SVG; it's shrunk automatically.

G5 Entry prices

Paid at the gate. Under-5s are always free. Until "Show prices" is ticked, visitors see "prices to be confirmed" – so you can put a proposal in first.

G6 Raffle

Tickets are sold on paper at the show, on the day only. Prizes, sales and the draw are on the Raffle page.

Then add a line to the register's change log if it isn't there already.
